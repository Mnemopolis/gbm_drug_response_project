01 · The Problem

High correlation on a random split is only half the story.

Glioblastoma (GBM) carries a median survival under 15 months, where response to first-line Temozolomide is governed by epigenetic silencing of the DNA-repair enzyme MGMT. We ask three questions to audit what a multimodal neural network actually learns.

RQ1 · Shortcut Audit

Drug–Gene Interaction vs. Potency Memorization

Does a 0.8744 random-split Spearman reflect learned drug–omics interactions, or can a zero-interaction additive baseline (ȳdrug + ȳcell − ȳ) match it by memorizing per-drug average toxicity?

Additive Baseline: 0.8752 (95.5% drug mean)
RQ2 · OOD Shift & Shrinkage

Biological OOD vs. Chemical Scaffold OOD

How does predictive performance change when generalizing to unseen patient tumors (Leave-Cell-Out) versus unseen Bemis–Murcko chemical scaffolds (Scaffold Split)?

Shrinkage σ(ŷ)/σ(y): 0.862 → 0.663 (Scaffold)
RQ3 · Metric Decoupling

Global Collapse vs. Within-Drug Ranking

When global Spearman drops to 0.2905 on unseen scaffolds, does the cell encoder still rank relative patient susceptibility within each novel compound?

Within-Drug Median rs: 0.530 (vs. 0.500 Random)

MD6329 Lecture 02 (Slides 73–81, 107, 123–124) & Lectures 05–06

What Counts as an Independent Data Example & Leakage-Free Scaling

As emphasized in Lecture 02 (Slides 107, 123–124), correlated measurements from the same biological entity (e.g., 200 slices from 1 patient CT volume) are not independent examples. Because our 5,107 assays span only 24 GBM cell lines and 243 drugs, naive random splitting leaks drug and cell identities—motivating our entity-disjoint Leave-Cell-Out and Murcko Scaffold splits, with StandardScaler, PCA, and MGMT median imputation fitted strictly on training cells (Lecture 02, Slides 73–81; Lecture 05, Slide 79; Lecture 06, Slides 15–20).

MD6329 Lecture 08 (Slides 3, 9–17, 29–33) & Lecture 10 (Slides 8, 14–15)

Heterogeneous Modalities & Joint (Intermediate) Fusion — Type I

Following the multimodal fusion taxonomy in Lecture 08 (Huang et al., 2020; Slides 29–33), raw input concatenation (Early Fusion, Slide 31) lets the 2048-D Morgan fingerprint overwhelm the 44-D cell omics vector (reduced from 19,138 genes via train-only PCA per Lecture 10, Slides 8, 14–15). Our Two-Tower BaselineMLP implements Joint Fusion — Type I (Slide 32): modality-specific encoders map drug and cell inputs into balanced 256-D representations before concatenation (512-D) and end-to-end backpropagation.

Why This State Matters

Fitting StandardScaler or PCA before splitting leaks test-set gene covariance into training representations. Freezing training moments on U-87 MG and T98G guarantees that held-out GAMG and LN-229 are evaluated strictly out-of-distribution.

06 · Scope & Responsible AI

Relative ranking yes, absolute dosing no

GDSC2 dose-response labels measure in-vitro 72-hour cell-line viability—they do not establish blood–brain barrier penetration, systemic toxicity, or clinical dosing safety in human Glioblastoma patients.

  • ✓ Valid Use Case (Biomarker Stratification): Preserved within-drug Spearman (0.530) under chemical OOD supports ranking candidate GBM cohorts by relative multi-omics susceptibility (MGMT status, PTEN/TP53 profile).
  • ✕ Prohibited Use Case (Uncalibrated Dosing): Severe variance shrinkage (σ(ŷ)/σ(y) = 0.663) on unseen scaffolds causes systematic potency offsets (−3.13 log error on Temozolomide); predictions must never be used directly for clinical dose selection.

MD6329 Lecture 11 Alignment (Slides 40–42 & 52–54): Grounds our OOD robustness audit (Nestor et al., 2019; Slide 41) and Model Cards for Model Reporting (Mitchell et al., 2019; Slides 52–54) separating intended clinical biomarker stratification from out-of-scope dosing.
